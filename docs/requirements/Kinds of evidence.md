# Kinds of evidence

An evidence note says what kind it is — interview, customer request, analytics, support
ticket, sales feedback, research, observation, experiment, market research — from a
vocabulary the vault configures, because what counts as evidence differs by product and by
team.

**Outcome** — Twelve customer requests and one analytics reading can be told apart without
opening either.
