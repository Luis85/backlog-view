# An idea becomes an opportunity

An idea grows into an opportunity by gaining structure — the problem, whose problem it is,
the impact expected, the evidence behind it, an owner — each an ordinary property under a
key the vault names. The note keeps its identity: this is the same note with more said
about it, not a new one.

**What makes it an opportunity is its lifecycle value, not the fields it has grown.** The
structure is why somebody moves it; the move is the state, written like any other state in
this view. That distinction is what other features can act on —
[[Promoting a candidate into the backlog]] offers promotion on a lifecycle value — where "has enough fields" would be a
rule appearing and disappearing as somebody types, and different in every reader's head.
Nothing here refuses the change for missing fields: the readiness checklist reports what is
thin, and a person decides.

**Outcome** — The difference between "someone suggested this" and "we understand this
problem" is visible in the vault.
