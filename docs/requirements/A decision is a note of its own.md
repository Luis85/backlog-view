# A decision is a note of its own

A decision note carries a date, a status and its reasoning as ordinary prose. It hangs from
nothing and holds nothing — it is a record, not work — and it is legible in Obsidian with
the plugin uninstalled.

**Outcome** — A product decision has a place to be written the moment it is made.
