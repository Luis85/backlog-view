# Moving a card on the map

**Outcome** — A card moves to another step or another release by drag, by keyboard or by
menu, and all three write the identical batch.

Two axes means two moves, and the register's rule is one host method per move: the drag, the
keyboard and the menu pick land on it, and it is the only place the batch is planned and the
only place the move is announced. [[Moving between horizons]] and [[Drag a card to a new state]] are the two
worked examples, and both of the moves here are the same shape as one of
them — a re-parent, and a single property write.
