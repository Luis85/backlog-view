# How strong the evidence is

An evidence note may carry a strength — weak, moderate, strong, or a number — under a
property the vault names. It is recorded by a person, never inferred, and it is what lets a
reader tell one anecdote from a study without reading both.

**Outcome** — The weight of an argument is visible beside it.
