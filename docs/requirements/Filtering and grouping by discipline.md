# Filtering and grouping by discipline

The tree, the board and the roadmap can each be narrowed to one discipline or grouped by
it, and an item shows its discipline as its own name — a chip, a group heading, a column —
never as a colour of its own.

**Outcome** — "Show me only the UX work" is a question the existing views answer, without a
second backlog to keep in step.

*Barebones. Every count under it reports what the base returned and never a context row,
which is the rule its use cases have to keep rather than restate.*
