For a selected item, its evidence summarized by kind — how many customer requests, support
incidents, interviews, analytics observations — derived from the linked notes and their own
kind property, never from a number anybody maintains by hand.

**Outcome** — The argument for a feature can be summarized in a line without losing what it
is made of.
