# Strategic types beside the ladder

Vision, theme, objective, outcome, OKR, JTBD and initiative are types a vault opts into,
sitting beside the work ladder rather than on it: they hold no rank among features and
enter no rollup. Which of them participate is configuration, and a vault that names none
still gets a backlog.

**Outcome** — A vault can keep exactly the strategic vocabulary it uses, and no more.
