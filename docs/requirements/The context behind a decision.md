# The context behind a decision

A selected decision shows what is there to show — the decision, its rationale, the
alternatives considered, the evidence, the items affected, and the outcome if one has been
written — from properties where they exist and from the note's own prose where they do not.
Nothing is required, and an absent section is absent rather than empty.

**Outcome** — The record answers the next reader's question instead of confirming that a
decision happened.
