# Cycle time between two states

The elapsed time between two configured transitions — ready to done, in progress to done,
discovery to validated — computed from the stamps those transitions wrote, over the items
that carry both. Items missing either stamp are excluded and counted, because a duration
averaged over the items that happened to be stamped is a fiction.

**Outcome** — How long things take can be measured, with its sample size on screen.
