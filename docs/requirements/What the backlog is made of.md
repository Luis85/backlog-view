# What the backlog is made of

Counts by type, state, objective, release, owner, tag and horizon — the composition of
whatever the base returned, each breakdown naming its population so two of them can be
compared without wondering whether they counted the same items.

**Outcome** — The make-up of the backlog is one glance rather than a hand count.
