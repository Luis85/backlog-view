# The strategy hierarchy

The strategic entities draw as their own tree — an objective holding outcomes holding
initiatives — a ladder of its own rooted at the top level, the way the test catalog's
`Test suite` → `Test case` is, and for the same reason: `parent` already decides level, rank,
rollup and focus for the work, and strategy is none of those.

**The tree stops where the work starts.** An epic is never a child of an initiative; it names
the strategic entity it serves in its own property, and this view reads that link backward to
show the work under each objective. So the two hierarchies meet at a property, and neither
one renumbers the other.

**Outcome** — Strategy has a shape a reader can navigate, and the backlog's ranking is
untouched by it.
