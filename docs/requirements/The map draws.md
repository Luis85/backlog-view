# The map draws

**Outcome** — Opening a storymap shows the journey: who is acting, what they are trying to
do, the steps in order, and the cards under each step.

The board and the roadmap each re-arrange the tree along one axis. This is the first
projection with two, and the horizontal one is a narrative rather than a value — which is
why the backbone reads three different things (a property, a cross-parent rank, and a rung's
children) to draw three rows that look alike.
