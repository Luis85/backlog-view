# Product Portfolio

**Several products, one perspective, no reorganization.** A portfolio view aggregates work
across products, domains or workstreams without any of those teams changing how their own
backlog is shaped.

**Outcome** — Someone overseeing several product areas can compare them on progress, size,
value and risk, and each area still runs its own backlog its own way.

## Why it is its own view

The levels above an epic are not more rungs on the same ladder — they are a grouping a
vault declares, and most vaults never declare one. Putting portfolio levels in the backlog
view's hierarchy settings would make every single-product vault configure something it does
not have.

## Definition of done, for anything under this epic

- Portfolio levels are configuration, and absent by default.
- Every rollup counts what the base returned, states its unit, and reports what it could
  not count rather than counting it as zero.
- Nothing here writes anything. It is a reading of work other views own.

## What this epic will not do

- **Manage people or budgets.** Aggregating work is not resource management, and the
  non-goals of the requirements document apply here hardest.
